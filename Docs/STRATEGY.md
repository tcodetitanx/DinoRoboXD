# DINO ROBO XD — Field Manual (unit strategy guide)

## The three currencies of war
1. **Feed** — dinos starve without it. Feed lines are military infrastructure.
2. **Power** — turrets and factories brown out together. Batteries before battles.
3. **Bandwidth** — your army size. Radar towers are barracks.

## Opening (Day 1–2)
Chop, mine stone, kiln up, get Beast Ken early — a single tamed **Trice** trivializes
early raids. Palisade the east approach first; Vex scouts from the east.
Keep the recon quad moving: finding amber groves and the first ore field beats
any build order.

## Mid game
- **Raptors screen, Trices hold, Ankys break.** Attack-move raptors ahead of your line
  to strip enemy screens, hold War Trices on Defensive at your wall gap, park a
  Cannon Anky behind them.
- Turrets are ammo-free but power-hungry: one coal_burner per 3 turrets, battery_bank
  per wall segment.
- **Raid rhythm**: every raid warning (klaxon, red ping) gives you 20s. Set a control
  group (say `5`) as your fire brigade; double-tap 5, A-click the ping.
- Camp-cracking package: 2 War Trice (front, Hold), 3 Gatling Raptors (behind),
  1 Wrecker Anky (A-move onto the skullgate), Jet Para for the speed horn.
  Loot every camp — salvaged neurochips are free cyber_modules.

## Stances
- **Aggressive**: melee dinos, raid defense brigades.
- **Defensive**: wall garrisons — they chase 6 tiles then walk back.
- **Hold**: artillery (Missile Trice, Cannon Anky), snipers, anything you don't want baited.

## Air
War Pteras counter Vex's gunships and siege columns (slow ground targets). Rotor Pteras
keep frontline turret walls fed with repair… no repair — with *distance*: build forward
storehouses so hauler runs are short.

## Late game & the push east
- Sail Spinos are the best escorts for daytime pushes (zero upkeep in sun).
- Crystal Stego walking beside your siege line = +25% turret/factory speed at forward bases.
- The Citadel ring: tesla spires chain-zap clumps — spread, send Wrecker Ankys on the
  spires first, shield_mech aura over the siege tanks, Red Rex eats the garrison,
  siege tanks eat the Citadel. Victory.

## Ten commandments
1. Feed troughs at the front, not the back.
2. Never research with an empty turret line.
3. A dino saved (medic bay) is a veteran earned.
4. Kill Basket Thieves on sight.
5. Scout before you wall — vents and rifts decide base sites.
6. Eggs beat conversions for cost; conversions beat eggs for time.
7. One drill_spider on a far patch beats three haulers walking there.
8. Vex escalates on *your* tech: tier up with a defense ready.
9. Bandwidth spent on scouts is never wasted.
10. The Citadel does not outlast a fed, powered, ranked army. Push before dawn.
