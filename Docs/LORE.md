# DINO ROBO XD — Lore Bible

## The Short Version

You are **Dr. Aris Kade**, chrono-physicist. Your identical twin, **Dr. Vex Kade**, stole your
Chrono-Displacement Core, used it, and vanished. When you rebuilt the core and followed his
signature through the rift, the math was off by five years — *his* five years.

You landed in the Cretaceous with a toolkit, a broken core, and a survival printer.
Vex landed in the same valley **five years earlier** — and he has not been idle.

He has enslaved the local dinosaur population with neural shackles and cybernetic grafts,
strip-mined the eastern ridges, and built the **Chrono-Citadel**: a machine that will punch
him back to the present *and seal the rift behind him*, stranding you here forever.

Your only way home is his way home. Out-build him. Out-breed him. Out-fight him.
Tear the Citadel down and take the core.

## The Twins

**Dr. Aris Kade (you)** — Field engineer first, scientist second. Believes machines should
serve life: your cybernetic grafts are symbiotic, powered by amber-resin biofuel, and your
dinos keep their instincts. Your art style: warm bronze, rounded plating, tribal scaffolds
growing into clean industry.

**Dr. Vex Kade (the Enemy)** — Arrived five years early and spent every day of it building.
His creed: *"Biology is a rough draft."* His dinos are lobotomized chassis wearing spiked
black iron. His territory is smokestacks, skull-gates, and tesla spires. He knows you
followed him. He has been expecting you. His raids start polite and end apocalyptic.

## The Valley of Teeth

A river valley bounded by volcanic ridges. You crash-land in the **western jungles**;
Vex rules the **eastern ash-fields** behind layered outposts. Between you: open plains
with wild herds, amber groves, ore fields, geothermal vents, and **chrono rifts** —
scars in spacetime that bleed crystallized time (**chrono shards**), the one resource
neither of you can synthesize.

## The Dinosaurs

Wild dinos are not monsters; they are *fauna*. Herbivore herds graze and stampede when
provoked. Predator packs hunt the herds — and your haulers. Every wild species can be
**tamed** (lured with feed, or hatched from recovered eggs) and then **cyber-converted**
at a Conversion Rig into a war-form or work-form. A converted dino is loyal unto death;
feeding them is your problem. An army marches on its feed troughs.

Vex's converted dinos ("**Shackled**") are recognizable by black plating and red optics.
Killing them is a mercy. Some wild dinos in the east still carry his broken shackles —
salvage the neurochips.

## Why Robots

Your survival printer speaks one language: robots. Small ones first — haulers, drill
spiders, recon quads. Big ones when the foundries come online — siege tanks, gunships,
mech brawlers. Robots don't eat, but they drink power; dinos eat, but they heal, breed,
and hit like a landslide. The winning base runs both.

## The Endgame

The Chrono-Citadel charges in stages. Every stage Vex completes, his raids escalate.
Destroy the Citadel and its guardian garrison, recover the **Displacement Core**,
and the rift is yours. Lose your **Field HQ** and the printer dies with it —
game over, forever a fossil.

## Timeline (for flavor text)

- **Y-5**: Vex arrives. Tames his first raptor with a cattle prod and a promise.
- **Y-4**: First shackle foundry. The eastern herds stop migrating.
- **Y-2**: The Citadel's foundation is poured over a chrono rift.
- **Y0, Day 1**: You arrive. Vex's recon drones photograph your crash site within the hour.
- **Y0, Day 2**: This is where the game begins.
